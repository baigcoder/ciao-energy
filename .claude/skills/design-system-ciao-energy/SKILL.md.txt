# Ciao Energy

## Mission
Deliver implementation-ready design-system guidance for Ciao Energy that can be applied consistently across the site.

## Brand
- Product/brand: Ciao Energy
- URL: https://www.ciaoenergy.com/
- Product surface: public, scroll-driven WebGL marketing and shop site

## Style Foundations
- Main font style: `font.family.primary=Geist`, `font.family.stack=Geist, Arial, sans-serif`, `font.size.base=14px`, `font.weight.base=300`, `font.lineHeight.base=21px`
- Typography scale: `font.size.xs=9.33px`, `font.size.sm=10.5px`, `font.size.md=12.25px`, `font.size.lg=14px`, `font.size.xl=15.75px`, `font.size.2xl=16px`, `font.size.3xl=42px`, `font.size.4xl=115.2px`
- Color palette: `color.text.primary=#ffffff`, `color.surface.base=#000000`, `color.surface.muted=#c9dff2`, plus one accent token per flavor
- Spacing scale: `space.1=3.5px`, `space.2=10.5px`, `space.3=12.6px`, `space.4=17.5px`, `space.5=21px`, `space.6=21.5px`
- Radius/shadow/motion tokens: `radius.xs=3.5px`, `radius.sm=7px` | `shadow.1=rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6) 0px 0px 20px 0px`, `shadow.2=rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2) 0px 0px 0px 1px` | `motion.duration.instant=200ms`, `motion.duration.fast=400ms`

## Accessibility
- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA
- Keyboard-first interactions required.
- Focus-visible rules required.
- Contrast constraints required.

## Rules: Do
- Use semantic tokens, not raw hex values, in components.
- Every component must define states: default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error.
- Responsive behavior and edge cases should be specified for every component family.
- Accessibility acceptance criteria must be testable in implementation.

## Rules: Don't
- Do not allow low-contrast text or hidden focus indicators.
- Do not introduce one-off spacing or typography exceptions.
- Do not use ambiguous labels or non-descriptive actions.

## Component Rule Expectations
- Include keyboard, pointer, and touch behavior.
- Include spacing and typography token requirements.
- Include long-content, overflow, and empty-state handling.
